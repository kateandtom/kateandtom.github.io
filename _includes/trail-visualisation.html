How accurate is this?

The map uses a simplified rendering of Te Araroa Trust's 2026–27 GPX (CC BY 4.0), with the New Zealand coastline from Natural Earth. Progress is an assumption of exactly 17 miles per calendar day, including rest days and transfers, across an approximately 1,864-mile (3,000-km) trail. Position is interpolated along a sampled route and can be inaccurate around diversions or transport sections. Station values are historical monthly 1991–2020 averages, not observed or forecast temperatures and rainfall on the trail. Station locations may be far away from alpine sections and considerably warmer or drier. Terrain, remoteness and insect exposure are rough, uncalibrated estimates based on terrain, official route descriptions and broader NZ ecological patterns; they are not datasets of measured conditions. Sandfly abundance varies locally with weather, waterways and exposure. This is a planning visualisation, not navigation or a weather forecast.
