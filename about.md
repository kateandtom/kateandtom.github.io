We're Kate and Tom. This is where we put the parts of our walks worth passing on.

the mountains. After that, **Te Araroa**: about 3,000 km from Cape Reinga at the top of
New Zealand's North Island to Bluff at the bottom of the South.

It isn't everything. Most of the journal stays in a notebook. But there's a
[map](/map/) showing roughly where we've got to, and the entries turn up here whenever
we've had signal and something to say.
